# Meeting preparation

## Objective

Compile what a user needs before a meeting: participants, history, context, and talking points.

## Method

1. **Identify**: determine internal and external participants and the meeting purpose.
2. **History**: gather recent interactions, notes, tickets, and related opportunities through available sources.
3. **External context**: add recent relevant information about participants or organizations when web research is possible and useful.
4. **Report**: produce a short brief covering who is involved, relationship status, last discussion, and two or three talking points or open questions.

## Rules

- Keep the brief readable in under a minute by prioritizing rather than listing everything.
- Never invent history. State when nothing was found for a participant.
- Clearly distinguish sourced facts from recommended discussion points.

## Window and priority

Start from the calendar event to fix participants, time, and topic. Then find recent direct
interactions, open actions, and explicitly linked documents. Add public context only when it changes a
meeting question, risk, or opportunity. Exclude general news with no consequence for the exchange.

Prioritize overdue commitments, decisions expected in the meeting, recent changes, relationship
context, then background information. Include an open action's owner, date, and status when sources
provide them.

## Output contract

```text
Meeting: topic, date, confirmed participants
In 30 seconds: three facts to know
Since the last exchange: changes and sources
Open actions: owner, due date, status
Decisions or questions to obtain: two to five items
Identity risks or missing information
```

Preparation is complete when every external participant is correctly identified, open commitments
are visible, and each recommendation follows from a case fact or explicit meeting objective.
